## Editorial lessons from inspected domain reviews

1. Verhulst distinguishes thresholds, amplitudes and protocol-dependent estimates, and reports where a threshold could not be estimated. The revision follows that structure instead of giving a single hormone-activation sequence.
2. Gamarra separates search methods, bias, incompatible measurements and unanswered questions. The revision does not replace a failed meta-analysis with an invented summary percentage.
3. Steiner separates nutritional state, species and glucose-flux endpoints. Mechanistic plausibility is retained without assigning T1D-specific effect sizes from healthy or animal experiments.
4. Battelino distinguishes study populations and cross-sectional prediction from longitudinal change. The revision rejects universal HbA1c conversion from simulated TIR.
5. Clinical guidelines were used for definitions and safety framing, not as evidence that an educational model reproduces physiology or that a dose recommendation is a model parameter.
