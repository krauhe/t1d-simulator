## Replacement paragraph

Locator: §5, `Gastric emptying as the principal kinetic determinant`, point 2.

> **Caloric and volumetric load.** Kwiatek et al. (2009) studied 16 healthy adults with iso-osmolar nutrient liquids spanning 200–800 mL and 200–400 kcal. MRI-derived gastric-content half-emptying time increased by 18 ± 6 min per additional 100 kcal in their fitted analysis, whereas larger volumes emptied faster. This protocol-specific relation does not establish a universal caloric clamp or a T1D meal coefficient. Gastric-content volume also includes secretion, so its half-emptying time is a composite endpoint.
