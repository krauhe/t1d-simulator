## Source checks that support, rather than overturn, the text

1. **§24, lines 2098 and 2118:** Mégarbane et al. 2007 original PDF p. 3 confirms six kinetic participants (two T1D, one T2D, three without diabetes), first-order terminal decline, median half-life 3.8 h (IQR 1.5–4.6), and four unfavourable outcomes among 25 ICU patients, including two deaths. The document correctly distinguishes 16% unfavourable from 8% mortality. The original's small, selected poisoning cohort limits generalisation, as the section notes.
2. **SC02 — ✅ FIKSET (2026-10-03, local; not committed), §§28–29:** Bergman et al. 1979 complete original PDF p. 1 confirms seven candidate models, conscious dogs, 100/200/300 mg/kg IV glucose and reproducibility at 300 mg/kg. BG-SCIENCE now identifies 13 IVGTTs in five dogs (nine tests in two dogs for model comparison; four further tests in three dogs), reports the approximately 24% coefficient of variation for the insulin-sensitivity index at 300 mg/kg, and removes the stale “abstract only/full text unavailable” labels. This is repeatability under an animal identification protocol, not T1D daily-life prediction.
3. **§29, lines 2606–2608 and 2614–2616:** Archive extraction locates statements about 300 virtual S2008 subjects, the S2013 component cohorts, and Wilinska's virtual parameters/overnight comparison. These particular numerical passages were **not** independently checked against their original page images in this bounded pass and should not be treated as certified findings. The Dalla Man 2014 single-meal limitation in EM05 *was* visually checked on original PDF p. 7.
