Sleep disruption model — nighttime interventions, chronic stress, and dawn amplification

Sleep disruption model showing how nighttime interventions are converted into metabolic effects. Actions between 22:00 and 07:00 create a new awakening only if more than 30 minutes have passed since the previous one. Each awakening adds a normally distributed sleep-loss increment to lostSleepHoursTonight. At 07:00, applySleepDebt() converts accumulated sleep loss into _pendingChronicStress with stressBoost = min(0.30, lostSleepHoursTonight * 0.06). The pending pool drains into chronicStressLevel with τ ≈ 30 min; chronicStressLevel then decays with t½ = 12 h. Lost sleep also amplifies the morning dawn amplitude by 12% per lost hour, while chronic stress adds up to 30% dawn amplification per unit stress and increases insulinResistanceFactor by 0.5 * chronicStressLevel.
