*Two-compartment subcutaneous absorption feeds plasma insulin, which drives
three effect compartments acting on glucose distribution, disposal, and
endogenous glucose production. **S1, S2**: subcutaneous depot stages (mU);
**I**: plasma insulin concentration (mU/L); **x1, x2, x3**: insulin-effect
compartments for distribution, disposal, and EGP suppression respectively;
**u(t)**: subcutaneous insulin input rate; **ka**: absorption rate constant;
**ke**: plasma elimination rate; **kb1..kb3**: effect-build rate constants.*
