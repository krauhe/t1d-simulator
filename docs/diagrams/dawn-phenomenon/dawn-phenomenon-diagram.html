Dawn phenomenon — hybrid HGP + circadian ISF model (Bolli & Gerich 1984 / simulator extension)

Simulator implementation of the dawn phenomenon (Bolli & Gerich, N Engl J Med 310:746-750, 1984), split into two coupled mechanisms. Mechanism 1 — hepatic glucose output: a sine-arc circadianCortisol signal (peak 08:00, amplitude 0.15) is added to stressMultiplier, raising EGP = EGP₀ · max(0, stressMultiplier − x3). Mechanism 2 — peripheral insulin sensitivity: circadianISF varies from 0.70 (08:00) to 1.20 (evening) and rescales kb1/kb2/kb3, so the same plasma insulin produces less peripheral effect at dawn. The amplitude baseAmp is recalculated at midnight as 0.15 · (1 + 0.12·h_lost) · (1 + 0.30·chronicStress), so poor sleep and residual cortisol amplify the next morning's rise. Combined effect at 08:00: stressMultiplier contribution 1.15 and circadianISF 0.70 yield approximately 64 % more insulin needed than at evening baseline. This is a simulator extension; the original Bolli/Gerich observation has been extended with a circadian ISF component informed by Schmidt et al. (2014).
