Carbohydrate absorption — Hovorka 2004 gut model with dynamic gastric emptying

Two-compartment gut absorption cascade from the Hovorka 2004 glucose-insulin model (Hovorka et al., Physiol Meas 25:905-920, 2004). D1 (stomach) empties into D2 (gut) at rate D1/τG; D2 delivers glucose to plasma at rate U_G = D2 · A_G / τG. The Hovorka 2004 original uses a fixed τG = 40 min. This simulator extends it with a mixture-dependent τG recomputed each tick from the carbohydrate composition currently inside D1: τG = carbBase · fiberMod · retentionMod + fatDelay, yielding 25–50 min for liquids and up to ~120 min for high-fiber, high-fat meals. The fat-delay term follows the Wolpert/Smart 2013 pizza-effect extension (Wolpert & Smart, J Diabetes Sci Technol 7:1154-1156, 2013).

Flow variables (arrows + their parameters)

U_Gglucose appearance rate into plasma = D2 · A_G / τG (mmol/min)
A_Gcarbohydrate bioavailability fraction (Hovorka 2004: 0.8)
τGdynamic gastric emptying time constant (min); range ~25 min liquid sugar to ~120 min high-fiber/fat meal; fallback τG = 40 min when stomachContentGrams < 0.1
carbBasechemical-complexity term = 25 + (1 − simpleFrac) × 25 (range 25–50 min)
fiberModsaturating fiber slowdown = 1 + 0.5 · ln(1 + fiber/2); fiber in grams inside D1
retentionModsolid-liquid modifier = 1 − 0.6 · (1 − retentionRatio); range 0.4–1.0; liquids empty faster
fatDelayfat-induced delay = 18 · ln(1 + fatIntestine/10) (min); Wolpert/Smart pizza-effect extension
simpleFracsimple-sugar fraction = stomachCarbsSimple / stomachCarbsTotal
retentionRatiosolid-content ratio = stomachRetentionWeight / stomachContentGrams
