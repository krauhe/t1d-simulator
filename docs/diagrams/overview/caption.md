*Simplified architecture of the T1D simulator. Player actions (food, insulin, activity) feed three input pathways. The Hovorka core couples plasma insulin and the glucose pools through insulin action (x1, x2, x3); stress hormones and exercise modulate the liver and peripheral disposal. Ketogenesis is gated by plasma insulin. The CGM sensor reads plasma glucose with delay. Each block is detailed in its own sub-model figure. **C/F/P**: carbohydrate / fat / protein in a meal; **U/Ub**: rapid (bolus) and basal insulin doses; **HR**: heart rate driving exercise dynamics; **D1, D2**: gut compartments (stomach, intestine); **S1/S2**: subcutaneous depot for rapid insulin; **S1b/S2b**: shadow cascade for basal insulin; **I**: plasma insulin (mU/L); **x1, x2, x3**: insulin effect compartments (transport, disposal, EGP suppression); **E1**: fast exercise effect (GLUT4-mediated muscle uptake); **E2**: slow exercise effect (post-exercise insulin sensitivity); **EGP**: endogenous (hepatic) glucose production; **Q1**: plasma glucose (BG = Q1 / V_G); **Q2**: peripheral glucose pool (muscle and adipose tissue); **BHB**: β-hydroxybutyrate (the dominant ketone body); **C**: interstitial glucose seen by the CGM sensor; **U_G**: glucose appearance from gut; **U_I, U_Ib**: rapid and basal insulin appearance in plasma; **F01c**: insulin-independent uptake (brain, RBC, etc.); **F_R**: renal glucose excretion above ~9 mmol/L; **ka_int**: interstitial diffusion rate (~0.073 /min, τ ≈ 14 min).*
