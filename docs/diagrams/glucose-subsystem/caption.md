*Two-compartment glucose distribution from Hovorka 2004, extended with one contraction-mediated uptake flux. Plasma glucose Q1 receives gut absorption U_G and hepatic production EGP, exchanges with peripheral Q2 through insulin-driven transport x1 and passive backflow k12·Q2, and is cleared by F01c and renal excretion F_R. Q2 loses glucose through x2-driven disposal and β·E1; E1 already contains normalized heart-rate and activity-type input. **Q1**: plasma glucose; **Q2**: peripheral glucose; **V_G**: glucose distribution volume; **U_G**: gut glucose appearance; **EGP**: hepatic glucose production; **F01c**: insulin-independent obligate glucose use; **F_R**: renal glucose excretion; **k12**: Q2-to-Q1 backflow constant; **x1**: insulin effect on transport; **x2**: insulin effect on disposal; **β·E1**: insulin-independent contraction-mediated uptake.*
