*Two-compartment glucose distribution from Hovorka et al. (Physiol Meas 25:905-920, 2004), extended with a direct, glucose-dependent contraction sink from Q1: plasma glucose receives U_G and EGP, exchanges with Q2 through x1-driven transport and k12 backflow, and supplies F01c, F_R and U_ex. Q2 loses glucose through x2-driven disposal, while delayed exercise sensitivity changes the upstream EC50 of x1 and x2; arrow equations show requested rates, with simultaneous outgoing fluxes from each glucose pool proportionally limited to its available mass per time step before updating either pool. **Q1**: plasma glucose in mmol; **Q2**: peripheral glucose in mmol; **V_G**: glucose distribution volume in L, approximately 0.16 × body weight in kg; **D2**: gut compartment supplying glucose; **U_G**: gut glucose appearance D2 / tau_G × splanchnicAbsorbMod in mmol/min, where tau_G is the absorption time constant and splanchnicAbsorbMod the blood-flow modifier; carbohydrate bioavailability A_G is already applied at D1 input; **EGP**: hepatic glucose production in mmol/min; **F01c**: insulin-independent obligate glucose use in mmol/min; **F_R**: renal glucose excretion in mmol/min; **k12**: Q2-to-Q1 backflow constant in min⁻¹; **x1**: insulin effect on transport in min⁻¹; **x2**: insulin effect on disposal in min⁻¹, giving requested disposal x2·Q2 in mmol/min; **U_ex**: net contraction-mediated Q1 disposal, requested as β·E1·G/5.5 mmol/min (contraction in code), independently of insulin-driven Q1→Q2 transport and reduced by the shared Q1 outflow limiter when necessary; _lastContraction records the realized flux; **β**: default beta = 0.78 × (body weight / 70 kg) mmol/min, an adapted simulator amplitude not clinically validated for this revised flux; **E1**: dimensionless contraction-response state including normalized heart-rate and activity-type input; **G**: plasma glucose Q1/V_G in mmol/L (BG), normalized by exerciseReferenceGlucose = 5.5 mmol/L, not an affinity constant or clinically validated parameter; U_ex is zero at G = 0.*
