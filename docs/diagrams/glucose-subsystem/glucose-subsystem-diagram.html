Glucose subsystem — Hovorka 2004 two-compartment model (modified)

Two-compartment glucose distribution from Hovorka et al. (Physiol Meas 25:905-920, 2004), extended with a direct, glucose-dependent contraction sink from Q1: plasma glucose receives U_G and EGP, exchanges with Q2 through x1-driven transport and k12 backflow, and supplies F01c, F_R and U_ex. Q2 loses glucose through x2-driven disposal, while delayed exercise sensitivity changes the upstream EC50 of x1 and x2; arrow equations show requested rates, with simultaneous outgoing fluxes from each glucose pool proportionally limited to its available mass per time step before updating either pool.

Variables

Compartment variables (boxes)

Q1plasma glucose (mmol); blood glucose BG = Q1 / V_G
Q2peripheral (intracellular) glucose (mmol) — muscle and adipose tissue
x1insulin effect on Q1→Q2 transport (min⁻¹, driven by plasma insulin I)
x2insulin effect on Q2 disposal (min⁻¹, driven by plasma insulin I); requested disposal rate x2·Q2 in mmol/min
D2gut compartment delivering glucose to plasma (see carb-absorption sub-model)

Flow variables (arrows + their parameters)

U_Gglucose appearance from gut absorption (mmol/min); rate = D2 / tau_G × splanchnicAbsorbMod, where tau_G is the absorption time constant and splanchnicAbsorbMod the blood-flow modifier; carbohydrate bioavailability A_G is already applied at D1 input
EGPendogenous (hepatic) glucose production (mmol/min) = EGP₀ · max(0, stressMultiplier − x3)
F01cinsulin-independent glucose uptake (mmol/min) — obligate brain use plus RBC; approximately 0.0097 × weight
F_Rrenal glucose excretion (mmol/min); active only when BG exceeds the renal threshold (~9 mmol/L)
k12rate constant for Q2→Q1 backflow (min⁻¹)
U_exnet contraction-mediated disposal from Q1, independently of insulin-driven Q1→Q2 transport; requested rate β·E1·G/5.5 mmol/min (contraction in code), reduced by the shared Q1 outflow limiter when necessary; _lastContraction records the realized flux
βdefault beta = 0.78 × (body weight / 70 kg) mmol/min; an adapted simulator amplitude, not clinically validated for this revised flux
E1dimensionless contraction-response state from the exercise subsystem; includes normalized heart-rate and activity-type input
Gplasma glucose concentration Q1/V_G in mmol/L (BG); normalization exerciseReferenceGlucose = 5.5 mmol/L is not an affinity constant or clinically validated parameter, and U_ex is zero at G = 0
V_Gglucose distribution volume (L) ≈ 0.16 × weight; converts Q1 from mmol to mmol/L for BG display
