Glucose subsystem — Hovorka 2004 two-compartment model

Two-compartment glucose distribution model from Hovorka et al. (Physiol Meas 25:905-920, 2004), extended with one contraction-mediated uptake flux. Plasma glucose Q1 receives gut absorption U_G and hepatic glucose production EGP, exchanges with peripheral Q2 through x1-driven transport and passive k12 backflow, and is cleared by F01c and renal excretion F_R. Q2 loses glucose through x2-driven disposal and the adapted Resalat flux β·E1; delayed exercise sensitivity changes the upstream EC50 of x1 and x2 rather than adding another multiplier here.

Variables

Compartment variables (boxes)

Q1plasma glucose (mmol); blood glucose BG = Q1 / V_G
Q2peripheral (intracellular) glucose (mmol) — muscle and adipose tissue
x1insulin effect on Q1→Q2 transport (dimensionless, driven by plasma insulin I)
x2insulin effect on Q2 disposal (dimensionless, driven by plasma insulin I)

Flow variables (arrows + their parameters)

U_Gglucose appearance from gut absorption (mmol/min); sourced from gut two-compartment D1→D2 cascade
EGPendogenous (hepatic) glucose production (mmol/min) = EGP₀ · max(0, stressMultiplier − x3)
F01cinsulin-independent glucose uptake (mmol/min) — obligate brain use plus RBC; approximately 0.0097 × weight
F_Rrenal glucose excretion (mmol/min); active only when BG exceeds the renal threshold (~9 mmol/L)
k12rate constant for Q2→Q1 backflow (min⁻¹)
β·E1contraction-mediated uptake (mmol/min); E1 already contains normalized heart-rate and activity-type input
V_Gglucose distribution volume (L) ≈ 0.16 × weight; converts Q1 from mmol to mmol/L for BG display
