*The CGM model adds four sources of deviation between true plasma glucose and the value shown to the player. (1) An interstitial compartment C follows G with first-order kinetics, ka_int = 0.073 /min (τ ≈ 14 min) — a low-pass filter that lags rising BG and over-reads falling BG, the dangerous case. (2) Gaussian sensor noise scales with BG (Box-Muller; σ ≈ 0.15 mmol/L at BG 5, σ ≈ 0.30 at BG 10), calibrated from ~34 000 real Libre 2 readings over a year. (3) A slow drift (sine wave, period 4–8 h, amplitude 0.3–0.7 mmol/L) modelling sensor bias. (4) Rare discontinuities (≈ 0.7 per simulated day, jumps up to ±2 mmol/L) for compression and calibration artefacts. **G**: true plasma glucose; G = Q1 / V_G (mmol/L); **C**: interstitial fluid glucose — what the CGM actually senses; **ka_int**: 0.073 /min — diffusion rate plasma → interstitium (τ ≈ 14 min); sensor noise: Gaussian, σ(BG) ≈ 0.03 · BG mmol/L; calibrated to Libre 2 in-vivo data; drift: sine wave, period U(4, 8) h, amplitude U(0.3, 0.7) mmol/L (sensor systematic bias); discontinuities: ~0.7 events/day, magnitude up to ±2 mmol/L (compression / calibration). Fingerstick alternative: direct plasma sample, ±5 % uncertainty, no interstitial delay.*
