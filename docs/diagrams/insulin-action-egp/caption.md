*Insulin pharmacokinetics and action subsystem of the Hovorka 2004 glucose-insulin model (Hovorka et al., Physiol Meas 25:905-920, 2004), with two simulator-specific extensions. CORE Hovorka structure: a two-stage subcutaneous absorption cascade (S1 → S2) feeds plasma insulin I, which drives three effect compartments x1 (transport), x2 (peripheral disposal), and x3 (EGP suppression). EXTENSIONS specific to this simulator: (1) a parallel basal shadow cascade S1b → S2b with fixed τ_Ib = 55 min that decouples basal absorption from bolus-specific τ_I variability; (2) a stressMultiplier term in the EGP equation (EGP = EGP₀ · max(0, stressMultiplier − x3)) that sums glycogen, gluconeogenesis, acute-stress, chronic-stress, dawn, and protein-glucagon contributions. Each kb_i is dynamically scaled by the ISF modifier (circadian × post-exercise × vasodilatation / insulin-resistance), so all three actions strengthen or weaken together with current insulin sensitivity. **S1**: rapid (bolus) SC depot — first absorption stage (mU); **S2**: rapid (bolus) SC depot — second absorption stage (mU); **S1b**: basal (long-acting) SC depot — first absorption stage (mU); shadow cascade with fixed τ = 55 min; **S2b**: basal (long-acting) SC depot — second absorption stage (mU); **I**: plasma insulin concentration (mU/L); **x1**: insulin effect on transport (Q1 → Q2); **x2**: insulin effect on disposal (Q2 oxidation); **x3**: insulin effect on EGP suppression; **Q1**: plasma glucose pool — receives EGP from liver, exchanges with peripheral tissues; **Q2**: peripheral (intracellular) glucose pool — site of insulin-mediated disposal; **U_I**: rapid absorption rate from S2 to plasma — formula: S2 / τ_I · pulseFactor; **U_Ib**: basal absorption rate from S2b to plasma — formula: S2b / τ_Ib · pulseFactor; **S1/τ_I**: inter-stage transfer rate, rapid cascade (S1 → S2); **S1b/τ_Ib**: inter-stage transfer rate, basal cascade (S1b → S2b); **τ_I**: rapid time constant (mean 55 min, CV ~25 % per injection); **pulseFactor**: heart-rate-dependent absorption acceleration (1.0 at rest, ~1.5 at HR 120); **k_e**: plasma insulin elimination rate constant (urine + hepatic clearance); **kb1, kb2, kb3**: build-up rate constants for the three effect compartments — scaled each tick by ISF modifier; **ka1, ka2, ka3**: decay rate constants for the three effect compartments; **stressMultiplier**: glycogenBaseline + gngBaseline + acute + chronic + dawn + protein-glucagon; **EGP₀**: basal hepatic glucose production (~0.0161 × weight mmol/min, T1D level).*
