Hovorka 2004 insulin subsystem (extended) — absorption, action, and EGP regulation

Insulin pharmacokinetics and action subsystem of the Hovorka 2004 glucose-insulin model (Hovorka et al., Physiol Meas 25:905-920, 2004), with two simulator-specific extensions. Core Hovorka structure: a two-stage subcutaneous absorption cascade (S1 → S2) feeds plasma insulin I, which drives three effect compartments x1 (transport), x2 (peripheral disposal), and x3 (EGP suppression). Extensions specific to this simulator: (1) a parallel basal shadow cascade S1b → S2b with fixed τ_Ib = 55 min that decouples basal absorption from bolus-specific τ_I variability; (2) a stressMultiplier term in the EGP equation (EGP = EGP₀ · max(0, stressMultiplier − x3)) that sums glycogen, gluconeogenesis, acute-stress, chronic-stress, dawn, and protein-glucagon contributions. Each kb_i is dynamically scaled by the ISF modifier (circadian × post-exercise × vasodilatation / insulin-resistance), so all three actions strengthen or weaken together with current insulin sensitivity.
