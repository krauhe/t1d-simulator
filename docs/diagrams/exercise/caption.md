*This pathway overview retains the E1 response from Resalat et al. (PLOS ONE 15:e0237511, 2020), while the insulin-independent, glucose-dependent disposal structure is inspired by Romeres et al. (2021), equations 4 and 11: U_ex removes glucose directly from Q1, independently of insulin-mediated transport through Q2. These are adapted simulator mechanisms, not a reproduction of either published model; β and the 5.5 mmol/L reference are not clinically validated, and glycogen remains a nonconserved capacity heuristic pending revision. **type, intensity**: selected activity profile and intensity; **HR**: current heart rate in beats per minute; **E1**: dimensionless response with dE1/dt = (exerciseInput − E1) / 5 min; **exerciseHepaticDrive**: bounded hepatic-output state with a 30-minute post-exercise decay half-life; **fast + early + late**: delayed insulin-sensitivity components with independent activity-specific scales and 15-minute, 4-hour and 18-hour decay half-lives, respectively; **muscleGlycogenGrams**: capacity heuristic that gates early delayed sensitivity, with replenishment neither limited by realized glucose uptake nor debited from Q1 or Q2; **pulseFactor**: heart-rate-dependent insulin-absorption multiplier, linked to a separate insulin-PK subsystem rather than one combined depot state; **Q1**: plasma-accessible glucose pool in mmol; **x1, x2 EC50 shift**: delayed muscle insulin-sensitivity effect, leaving hepatic x3 unchanged; **exerciseInput**: max(0, (HR − HR_rest) / HR_rest) × contractionUptakeScaling; **U_ex**: requested net contraction-mediated disposal β·E1·G/5.5 in mmol/min (contraction in code), applied once as a Q1 sink and zero at G = 0; simultaneous outgoing fluxes from each glucose pool are proportionally limited to its available mass per time step, and realized U_ex is stored as _lastContraction; **β**: default beta = 0.78 × (body weight / 70 kg) mmol/min, an adapted amplitude not clinically validated for this revised flux; **G**: Q1/V_G in mmol/L, normalized by exerciseReferenceGlucose = 5.5 mmol/L, not a measured affinity constant; **EGP_ex**: exerciseHepaticDrive times liver-glycogen availability; **glycogenUseScaling**: activity-specific glycogen-use scale; **U_I, U_Ib**: insulin appearance rates accelerated by pulseFactor.*
