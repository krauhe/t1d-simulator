*The contraction pathway adapts the E1 equation from Resalat et al. (PLOS ONE 15:e0237511, 2020): normalized heart-rate excess is filtered with a 5-minute time constant and enters Q2 once as β·E1. Exercise-specific hepatic output, delayed insulin sensitivity, muscle-glycogen turnover and pulseFactor are explicit simulator extensions calibrated or constrained against exercise literature. **type, intensity**: selected activity profile and intensity; **HR**: current heart rate; **E1**: dimensionless contraction-response state; **exerciseHepaticDrive**: bounded exercise-specific hepatic-output state; **fast + early + late**: continuous delayed insulin-sensitivity components with independent activity-specific scales; **muscleGlycogenGrams**: intracellular bookkeeping pool that gates early PEIS without adding a second plasma-glucose drain; **pulseFactor**: heart-rate-dependent insulin-absorption multiplier; **Q1**: plasma-accessible glucose; **Q2**: peripheral glucose; **S1/S2, S1b/S2b**: rapid and basal subcutaneous insulin depots; **x1, x2 EC50 shift**: delayed muscle insulin-sensitivity effect; **exerciseInput**: normalized heart-rate excess times contractionUptakeScaling; **β·E1**: contraction-mediated glucose uptake applied once, with β scaled around the 70 kg reference; **EGP_ex**: exerciseHepaticDrive times liver-glycogen availability; **glycogenUseScaling**: activity-specific glycogen-use scale; **U_I, U_Ib**: insulin appearance rates accelerated by pulseFactor.*
