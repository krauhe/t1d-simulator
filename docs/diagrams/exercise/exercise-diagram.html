Exercise — adapted simulator mechanisms (Resalat 2020; Romeres 2021)

This pathway overview retains the E1 response from Resalat et al. (PLOS ONE 15:e0237511, 2020), while the insulin-independent, glucose-dependent disposal structure is inspired by Romeres et al. (2021), equations 4 and 11: U_ex removes glucose directly from Q1, independently of insulin-mediated transport through Q2. These are adapted simulator mechanisms, not a reproduction of either published model; β and the 5.5 mmol/L reference are not clinically validated, and glycogen remains a nonconserved capacity heuristic pending revision.

This is a pathway overview, not a complete ODE-state diagram. The dotted insulin-PK box refers to a separate subsystem, not a single combined depot state. Dashed arrows show control signals; the solid U_ex arrow shows glucose leaving Q1. Q2 transport and the other glucose fluxes are outside this figure's scope.
