Exercise model — Resalat E1 pathway with simulator extensions

The contraction pathway adapts the E1 equation from Resalat et al. (PLOS ONE 15:e0237511, 2020): normalized heart-rate excess is filtered with a 5-minute time constant and enters Q2 once as β·E1. Exercise-specific hepatic output, delayed insulin sensitivity, muscle-glycogen turnover and pulseFactor are explicit simulator extensions calibrated or constrained against exercise literature.
