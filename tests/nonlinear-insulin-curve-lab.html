Nonlinear insulin curve lab

Experimental N5-style plasma-insulin staircase clamps. This page is for analysis only: it does not change the simulator model.

Question: what parameter changes would be needed before the model shows a small but visible functional nonlinearity in BG during inactive plasma-insulin clamps? The current model uses EC50_muscle = 55 mU/L and hillN_muscle = 1.5. Here we compare steeper/right-shifted muscle curves against the current curve. The final two experimental variants also reduce early liver EGP suppression; that is not a proposal, just a diagnostic test of what would be required.
